New features: list, due filter, walk planner, your visits

For approval before building. Dashed orange outlines mark what's new. Library names other than Flinders St Baptist Courtyard Library and all the numbers are sample data. The walk and circles screens use a simplified drawing of central Adelaide (the real ones use the map).

1. Getting there from the map

New Three more map buttons

Under "show my location" and above "?": List (libraries near you), Due only (filter) and Your visits (streak, stats and circles). Same round buttons as now.

New Due only

Hides libraries visited in the last 30 days, so only amber (never visited) and ajar (visited over 30 days ago) ones remain. The button turns amber and a pill says what's hidden; "Show all" turns it off. Remembered between launches.

Cleared (filled): the largest circle around one of your visited libraries with no unvisited library inside; of those, the one holding the most visited libraries. Reach (dashed): the smallest circle around every library you've visited. Visit the amber library just outside the cleared circle and it grows.
