Help test Novel Route

Novel Route is being tested on Google Play before it goes out to everyone. You'll need an Android phone and the Google account you use for the Play Store. Three steps, about two minutes:

  1. Join the testers group

    This is what gives your Google account access. Without it, step 2 won't work.

    Join the group
    Did it work? Once you've joined, the group page no longer shows a Join group button. If it asked you to sign in, use the Google account your Play Store uses.
    Yes, I've joined Not yet
    You're in. Google Play can take up to an hour to catch up. You can close this page: when you come back, carry on from step 2.

  2. Become a tester on Google Play

    Press Become a tester on the page that opens. If it says "Item not found", check you joined the group with the same Google account as your Play Store; if you did, Google Play hasn't caught up yet: try again a bit later.

    Open the testing page

  3. Install Novel Route

    Then open it wherever you are. Every street library in Australia is already on the map.

    Get it on Google Play
