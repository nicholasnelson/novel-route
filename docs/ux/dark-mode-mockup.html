Dark mode, following the phone

When the phone is set to dark (or switches at night), the app follows: Mapbox's night lighting for the map, and the website's dark colours for every card, panel and button. Light mode is unchanged. There's no setting in the app; it just follows Android.

1. Map and nearby card

Mapbox Standard's "night" lighting. The library markers keep their colours (they stand out more). Group badges get a dark centre so they don't glare.
