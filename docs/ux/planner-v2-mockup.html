For approval before building. Dashed orange outlines mark what's new or changed. Names and distances are examples, set out like your case: one library 1 km east, one 1 km west, and three more about 1.2 km west.

1. Tapping a library opens its details straight away

New Drawer, as it opens

No small card first. The drawer slides up with everything you need: the visit button, a past visit and the four actions. The map stays visible above it, with the library ringed. Tap the map or swipe down to close.
