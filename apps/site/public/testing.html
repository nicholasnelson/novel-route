Help test Novel Route

Novel Route is nearly ready for Google Play. Before it goes out to everyone, it needs a few street library regulars to use it for a couple of weeks and say what works and what doesn't.

Google asks new apps to be tested by a small group for at least 14 days, so sticking with it for a fortnight really helps.

Not on Google Play just yet. Join the testers group now, and you'll get an email through it as soon as Novel Route is ready to install.

Join in

You'll need an Android phone and the Google account you use for the Play Store.

  1. Join the testers group. Open the group and choose Join group. It's only used to give you access and the odd short update, and members can't see each other.
  2. Become a tester on Google Play (once the group's email says it's ready). Open the testing page and choose Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, give it an hour and try again.
  3. Install Novel Route from the link on that page, then open it wherever you are.

What to try

  • Do the street libraries you know show up where you'd expect?
  • Drop in on a few and record your visits.
  • Pan somewhere new and see how quickly it fills in.
  • Anything that confuses you, even for a second, is worth mentioning.

Good to know

  • The first time you look at a new area, libraries can take a few seconds to appear.
  • Library details come straight from Street Library Australia. If one is missing or in the wrong spot, its keeper can update it there.
  • Your visits stay on your phone. The testers group never sees them.

Sharing what you think

  • In the app, tap ? then Send feedback. It adds your app version and phone model, so a report can be matched to what you saw. It never includes your location.
  • Or email hello@novelroute.app.
  • About a week in, a five-question survey will arrive through the group.

Leaving

Leave the group or choose Leave the program on the Google Play testing page whenever you like. Thanks for helping.
