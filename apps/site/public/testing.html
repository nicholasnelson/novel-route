Help test Novel Route

Novel Route is being tested on Google Play before it goes out to everyone. You'll need an Android phone and the Google account you use for the Play Store. Two steps, about two minutes:

Please take it slowly

Google's sign-up has a couple of confusing screens, and a few people have got stuck. Read each step before tapping, and if you see one of these, there's a quick fix:

  • “You don't have permission to access this content” when joining the group (step 1)
  • “Item not found” on Google Play (step 2)

  1. Join the testers group

    This is what gives your Google account access to the test. Without it, step 2 won't work.

    Join the group
    Seeing “You don't have permission to access this content”?

    That page means you're not signed in to Google there. It often happens when the link was opened from Facebook or Messenger. Either:

    • tap Sign in at the top right of that page and use the Google account your Play Store uses, then tap Join group; or
    • open this page in your phone's normal browser (such as Chrome) and start again.

    Did it work? Once you've joined, the group page no longer shows a Join group button. If it asked you to sign in, use the Google account your Play Store uses.

    You're in. Google Play can take up to an hour to catch up. You can close this page: when you come back, carry on from step 2.

    Still stuck? Email hello@novelroute.app and we'll sort it out together.

  2. Get Novel Route on Google Play

    Open Google Play's testing page for Novel Route, then:

    Open the testing page
    1. Tap Become a tester.
    2. When it says You are a tester, tap download it on Google Play.
    3. Tap Install. While it's being tested, the app is called Novel Route (Internal Early Access).
    Seeing “Item not found”?

    Google Play doesn't know you're a tester yet. That's one of two things:

    • You haven't joined the group yet. Go back to step 1.
    • You joined just now. Google can take up to an hour to catch up. Have a cuppa and try again later.

    Still stuck? Email hello@novelroute.app and we'll sort it out together.

Want to go through the steps again?

What to try

  • Check the street libraries you know show up where you'd expect.
  • Tap a library you drop in on and press I visited.
  • Plan a walk: in the ••• menu, tap Plan a walk, tick a few libraries (or pick them on the map), then try following it.
  • Have a look at My visits in the same menu for your streak.
  • Pan somewhere new and see how quickly it fills in.
  • Tell us anything that confuses you, even for a second.

Please keep it for two weeks

Google asks new apps to be tested by a small group for at least 14 days before anyone else can get them, so keeping Novel Route installed for a fortnight really helps.

Good to know

  • The first time you look at a new area, libraries can take a few seconds to appear.
  • Library details come straight from Street Library Australia. If one is missing or in the wrong spot, you can let Street Library Australia know through their website.
  • Your visits stay on your phone.

Sharing what you think

  • In the app, tap •••, then ?, then Send feedback. It adds your app version and phone model, so a report can be matched to what you saw. It never includes your location.
  • Or email hello@novelroute.app.
  • Or tap Write feedback on Novel Route's page in the Play Store. Only we can see it.

Leaving

Leave the group or choose Leave the program on the Google Play testing page whenever you like. Thanks for helping.
